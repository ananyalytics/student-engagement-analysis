4 COMPARISON BASES IN ANALYTICS:
1. Primary benchmark
2. trend benchmark
3. relative benchmark
4. internal benchmark

- Is engagement good or bad? Compared to what?

Engagement is neither good nor bad in isolation. It must be evaluated relative to business outcomes, historical performance, peer segments, and defined benchmarks.

Primary benchmark - engagement vs business outcome

Higher engagement → higher conversion to paid?
Higher engagement → longer subscription duration?
Lower engagement → higher churn?

operationalization:
average engagement of paid vs free users
engagement of retained vs churned users
engagement quartiles vs revenue contribution (plot)

Trend Benchmark - engagement vs time

Current month vs previous month
Pre- and post-subscription periods
Early lifecycle vs mature users

Relative benchmark - engagement vs peer segments

Subscription plans
Courses / student types
New vs returning users

- Does engagement lead to retention, upgrades, or churn?
