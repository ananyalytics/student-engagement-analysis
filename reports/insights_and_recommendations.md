# 📊 Primary Benchmark Analysis  
### Student Activity & Subscription Health Overview

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 1. High Churn Rate (34.6%)
- Approximately **1 in 3 users are churning**, indicating a significant retention issue.

### 2. Low Engagement Drives Churn
- Users in the **low engagement segment have more churned users than active users**.
- This segment is the **highest risk group** and a primary contributor to churn.

### 3. Medium Engagement is the Core User Base
- The majority of users fall under **medium engagement**.
- This segment shows relatively better retention and stability.

### 4. High Engagement Users are Highly Retained
- **Minimal to no churn** observed in the high engagement segment.
- These users represent the **most loyal and valuable group**.

### 5. Satisfaction Correlates with Engagement
- High & Medium Engagement: ~6.8 average satisfaction  
- Low Engagement: ~6.0 average satisfaction  
- Indicates a **clear drop in user experience** for low engagement users.

### 6. Balanced Subscription Distribution
- Free: ~38%  
- Basic: ~33%  
- Premium: ~29%  

---

## 🎯 Recommendations

### 1. Improve Low Engagement Segment
- Focus on reducing churn at the entry level.
- Key actions:
  - Enhance onboarding experience  
  - Introduce engagement nudges/reminders  
  - Simplify initial user journey  

### 2. Move Medium → High Engagement
- Largest user segment = biggest opportunity.
- Strategies:
  - Gamification (streaks, rewards)  
  - Progress tracking  
  - Personalized content recommendations  

### 3. Retain High Engagement Users
- Treat as high-value users.
- Actions:
  - Loyalty programs  
  - Exclusive features or early access  
- Goal: **Retention + Upselling**

### 4. Address Satisfaction Drop in Low Engagement
- Significant satisfaction gap (~0.9 points).
- Actions:
  - Collect targeted feedback  
  - Identify usability issues  

### 5. Prioritize Engagement Over Pricing
- Balanced subscription distribution indicates churn is **behavior-driven**.
- Focus on improving engagement before optimizing pricing strategies.

---

## 🧠 Conclusion

The primary issue is concentrated in the **low engagement segment**, where both churn and satisfaction are weakest.  
Improving engagement at this stage will directly impact **retention, satisfaction, and overall user health**.

# 📊 Trend Benchmark Analysis  
### Overview of Student Behavioral Shifts

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 1. Strong Increase in Engagement After Subscription
- Avg Time Before Subscription: **28.22**
- Avg Time After Subscription: **49.64**
- Clear **~75% increase in time spent**, showing subscriptions significantly boost usage.

### 2. Moderate Overall Engagement Level
- Avg Engagement Score: **2.48 / 4**
- Indicates users are **not fully engaged**, sitting around mid-level activity.

### 3. Long Subscription Duration
- Avg Subscription Days: **307 days**

### 4. Monthly Engagement Fluctuations Exist
- Time spent varies significantly across months.
- Peaks and drops indicate **inconsistent user behavior**, not a steady engagement pattern.

### 5. Drop in Engagement Towards Later Months (Some Years)
- Certain trends (especially later months) show **declining time spent**. 

### 6. Overall Avg Engagement Time is Moderate
- Avg Engagement Hours: **40.80**

---

## 🎯 Recommendations

### 1. Leverage Post-Subscription Momentum
- Since engagement spikes after subscription:
  - Introduce **feature highlights immediately after purchase**
  - Use **guided onboarding for paid users**

### 2. Improve Pre-Subscription Experience
- Pre-subscription engagement is significantly lower
- Actions:
  - Offer **free trials or previews**
  - Improve perceived value before conversion  

### 3. Stabilize Monthly Engagement
- Address fluctuations by:
  - Regular content updates  
  - Notifications/reminders during low-activity periods  

### 4. Prevent Long-Term Engagement Drop
- Keep long-term users from becoming inactive
- Strategies:
  - New features over time  
  - Personalized recommendations  

### 5. Push Users Beyond Medium Engagement
- Current engagement score (2.48) is average
- Actions:
  - Gamification (badges, streaks)  
  - Progress tracking  

---

## 🧠 Conclusion

Subscriptions clearly drive higher engagement, but overall user activity remains **moderate and inconsistent over time**.  
The focus should be on **improving pre-conversion experience and maintaining long-term engagement stability**.
